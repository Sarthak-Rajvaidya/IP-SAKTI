# Why classification comes before IP strategy

A formulation's classification — classical, proprietary, new/non-classical,
phytopharmaceutical, food/nutraceutical, or cosmetic — determines which
regulatory pathway applies and, downstream, which IP protections are even
realistically available. A classical formulation prepared exactly per a
recognised classical text is generally not patentable on its own (it is
prior art / traditional knowledge), whereas a proprietary formulation with
genuine modification has a stronger, though not guaranteed, patentability
position. This is why IP-SAKTI's assistant always tries to establish product
context and classification before answering a patentability or protection
question — the same question ("can I protect this?") has a materially
different answer depending on classification.
