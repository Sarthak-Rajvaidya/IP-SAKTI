# TKDL and prior-art searches — explanatory reference (DEMO)

The Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL) is a documentation
initiative that catalogues traditional-medicine knowledge, including
Ayurveda, from classical texts and public-domain sources, in a
searchable, prior-art-usable format for patent examiners internationally.

For an innovator evaluating patentability of an Ayurvedic formulation, a
TKDL-style prior-art search is a practical first step: it helps identify
whether the exact formulation, or an aggregation of its properties, is
already documented as traditional knowledge, which is relevant to the
Section 3(p) exclusion under Indian patent law and to novelty assessments
generally.

A prior-art search does not itself grant or deny protection — it produces
evidence that informs a patentability opinion, ideally reviewed by a
qualified patent professional before filing.

This document is a general explanatory reference for the IP-SAKTI prototype
and is not an official TKDL publication.
