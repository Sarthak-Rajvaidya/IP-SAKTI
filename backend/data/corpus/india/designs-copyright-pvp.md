# Supplementary IP routes — explanatory reference (DEMO)

This is a project-reference explanatory note, not enacted statutory text.

Beyond patents and trademarks, three additional IP routes are commonly
relevant to Ayurveda businesses:

- Design protection can cover the distinctive visual appearance of a
  product — bottle shape, packaging form, or applicator design — where the
  shape has independent commercial value and is not dictated purely by
  function. Filing before public disclosure is important, since disclosure
  can affect novelty.
- Copyright automatically protects original expressive works — patient
  literature, packaging artwork, training material, brand storytelling — but
  does not protect the underlying formulation or idea, only its specific
  expression.
- Plant Variety Protection is relevant where a distinct, uniform and stable
  plant variety or cultivar (for example, a specific breeding line of a
  medicinal plant used as a raw material) has been developed, and is
  separate from protecting the formulation that uses that plant as an
  ingredient. Farmers' rights and benefit-sharing considerations can apply
  under this framework.
