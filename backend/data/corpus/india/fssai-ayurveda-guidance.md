# FSSAI and Ayurveda-adjacent food products — explanatory reference (DEMO)

This is a project-reference explanatory note, not an official FSSAI
publication.

Products positioned as food, dietary supplements, or "Ayurveda-Aahar" rather
than as medicines generally fall under food-safety regulation administered
by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI), rather than
AYUSH drug licensing. The distinction matters because it changes the
permitted health claims, labelling requirements, and the licensing authority
a business needs to engage with. A product that makes therapeutic claims
typically needs to be evaluated as a drug rather than a food product, which
has different classification and IP implications.
