# TRIPS Agreement — explanatory reference (DEMO)

This is a project-reference explanatory note, not the treaty text itself.

The TRIPS Agreement sets minimum standards for intellectual property
protection among WTO member states, including patents. It generally
requires patents to be available for inventions that are new, involve an
inventive step, and are capable of industrial application, while allowing
members some flexibility to exclude certain subject matter (for example, on
public-order, morality, or public-health grounds, subject to conditions).

For an Ayurveda business exporting or filing internationally, this means
that individual national/regional patent offices (US, EU, etc.) will apply
their own implementing law on top of this baseline — patentability findings
in India do not automatically transfer, and traditional-knowledge-related
exclusions can differ meaningfully between jurisdictions.
