# International filing systems — explanatory reference (DEMO)

This is a project-reference explanatory note, not treaty text.

Four WIPO-administered systems are commonly relevant once an Ayurveda
business looks beyond India:

- The Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT) allows a single international patent
  application to preserve the option of seeking protection in many member
  countries, deferring (not avoiding) the cost of individual national-phase
  filings.
- The Madrid System allows a single international trademark application,
  based on a home registration, to be extended to multiple member
  countries.
- The Hague System provides a similar single-application route for
  industrial design registration across member countries.
- The Budapest Treaty addresses international deposit of micro-organisms
  for patent-procedure purposes, which can be relevant for certain
  fermentation- or microbe-based formulation processes.

None of these systems grant rights directly — they streamline the filing
process; actual grant/registration still depends on each national or
regional office's substantive examination.
