# CBD & Nagoya Protocol — explanatory reference (DEMO)

This is a project-reference explanatory note, not the treaty text itself.

The Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD) and its Nagoya Protocol set out
an international framework for access to genetic/biological resources and
fair and equitable sharing of benefits arising from their use, including a
requirement for users to comply with the access-country's domestic ABS
legislation (such as India's Biological Diversity Act) when using resources
sourced from that country, even when commercialising internationally. Export
markets such as the EU implement their own domestic legislation giving
effect to Nagoya-style due-diligence obligations, which can require evidence
of legal access and benefit-sharing compliance in the country of origin as
part of market access.
