# WIPO Traditional Knowledge provisions — explanatory reference (DEMO)

This is a project-reference explanatory note describing draft/proposed
material, not finalised treaty text — it should be labelled as such whenever
referenced.

WIPO's Intergovernmental Committee on Intellectual Property and Genetic
Resources, Traditional Knowledge and Folklore has, over an extended period,
worked on draft international instruments addressing how patent systems
should treat traditional knowledge and genetic resources, including possible
disclosure-of-origin requirements for patent applicants. As of the last
update reflected in this demo corpus, this work remains at the draft/proposed
stage rather than being settled binding international law, and the current
status should always be verified directly with WIPO before being relied on.
